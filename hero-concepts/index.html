Four ways to open the site.

The current home page opens on a particle field and a motor lesson read out by the browser's own voice. These four are alternatives. Each one is a complete home page: the first screen is a live 3D scene, drawn in the browser as you watch, and the rest of the page follows below it. Nothing on the current site has changed.

All four use the nav, type and colours of the current site, and each one carries on into the same sections below the fold. There is no voiceover anywhere: the captions carry the explanation, and the 3D does the rest. On a laptop each scene runs at the screen's refresh rate; on a weak machine the pages drop their resolution rather than their frame rate.
