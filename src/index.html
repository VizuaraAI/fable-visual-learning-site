Coulomb's law PYQ 2019 · 2014 · 2011

The electrostatic force between two point charges is directly proportional to the product of the charges and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.

F = 14πε0 · |q1||q2|r2
k= 1/4πε0 = 9 × 109 N·m2·C−2 ε0= 8.85 × 10−12 C2·N−1·m−2 SI unitnewton (N)

In vector form

Force is a vector, so the law is written along the unit vector joining the charges. Because r̂12 = −r̂21, the two forces come out equal and opposite, which is Newton's third law. Vectors are set in bold.

F12 = −F21

Properties of charge PYQ 2021 · 2020 · 2018 · 2017

Charge is additive, conserved, quantised (Q = ne, with e = 1.6 × 10−19 C) and invariant: it does not depend on whether the body is at rest or moving.
